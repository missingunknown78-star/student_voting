Voting Guidelines

📌 Purpose of the Election

The Student Government Election allows officially enrolled students to select their leaders in a fair, transparent, and secure manner using the online voting system.

🗳️ Voting Rules

  • Each student is allowed to vote only once.
  • Votes submitted cannot be changed or canceled.
  • Only officially registered and verified students can participate.
  • Sharing of accounts or voting on behalf of others is strictly prohibited.

📝 How to Vote

  1. Log in using your registered student account.
  2. Go to the Available Elections section.
  3. Select your preferred candidates for each position.
  4. Review your choices carefully.
  5. Click Submit Vote to finalize your ballot.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Your vote is confidential. The system does not display individual voting choices to administrators or other students. All votes are securely stored and counted automatically.

⚠ Important Reminders

  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection before voting.
  • Do not refresh or close the browser while submitting your vote.
  • Report any technical issues immediately to the election committee.

🔑 Fingerprint Login

To enable fingerprint login for faster access, go to your Profile page and register your fingerprint. Once registered, you can log in securely using your fingerprint on supported devices.
